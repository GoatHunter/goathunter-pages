Privacy Policy for Astro Boat

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Summary: Astro Boat is a standalone, offline retro arcade space combat game. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or device information whatsoever.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Astro Boat (Package: com.raskin.astroboat), developed and published by GoatHunter ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our players. Because Astro Boat operates as an offline single-player experience, your personal information never leaves your device.

2. Information Collection and Usage

Astro Boat does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or any personal account credentials.
  • No Location Data: We do not track, query, or collect your GPS, IP address, or approximate physical location.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not collect MAC addresses, IMEI, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not contain third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to track player actions or behavior.

3. Local Device Storage

Astro Boat saves limited game progression data strictly on your local device storage, including:

  • High scores leaderboard initials and points (e.g., local arcade ranking)
  • Audio settings (sound effects and music volume preferences)
  • Highest sector unlocked

This data is saved locally within the application's isolated sandbox storage (user:// storage space) and is never transmitted to any external server. You can erase all game data at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device's Android settings.

4. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Astro Boat contains no third-party advertisements and no in-app purchases or payment processing SDKs.

5. Third-Party Services and External Links

The application does not embed or communicate with any third-party APIs, ad networks, tracking SDKs, or social networks.

6. Permissions

Astro Boat requires no sensitive device permissions. It does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage permissions, phone status, or calendar. The only permission declared in the manifest is optional handheld vibration feedback (Haptic Feedback) for tactile arcade controls.

7. Children’s Privacy

Astro Boat does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, but it is suitable for audiences of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage players to review this page periodically for any updates.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the developer contact details provided on the Google Play Store application page.
